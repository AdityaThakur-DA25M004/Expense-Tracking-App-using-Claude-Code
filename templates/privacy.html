Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and a hashed password. When you use Spendly, we collect the expense data you enter: amounts, categories, dates, and optional descriptions.

We do not collect payment information, government-issued IDs, or any sensitive financial account details.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate and improve Spendly. Specifically, we use it to display your expense history, generate summaries and breakdowns, and maintain your account.

We do not use your data for advertising, sell it to third parties, or share it with anyone outside of what is necessary to run the service.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data from unauthorized access.

You can delete your account at any time. Upon deletion, all your personal data and expense records will be permanently removed from our systems within 30 days.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly uses Google Fonts to serve typefaces used in the interface. This means your browser makes a request to Google's servers when you visit the site. No personal expense data is shared with Google or any other third party.

We do not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking pixels.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at privacy@spendly.app.
